  <h3>How do you build for small?</h3>
  <p>
    Use devtools to simulate a small device. THen scale down you elements for
    that screen.
  </p>
  <h3>How do you avoid overflow of elements?</h3>
  <p>
    Be careful with using position: absolute. It might make the html bigger then
    intended.
  </p>
  <h3>How do you make the site scale for different sizes</h3>
  <p>Use min and max.</p>
