  <h3>What is the work model?</h3>
  <dl>
    <dt>Analyze</dt>
    <dd>
      Look at the assignment and make a breakdown and bulletpoints on what has
      the project has to have.
    </dd>
    <dt>Design</dt>
    <dd>Schets out the pages and there the structure with the content.</dd>
    <dt>Build</dt>
    <dd>Make the repository and write HTML, CSS</dd>
    <dt>Deploy/integrate</dt>
    <dd>
      Attach it to the other systems or pages it has and then publish you r site
      for all to see.
    </dd>
    <dt>Test</dt>
    <dd>
      Let others use your website and let them review your code. then collect
      all the feedback and go back to Analyze
    </dd>
  </dl>
  <h3>How to Setup github page?</h3>
  <p>
    Make a repository or fork a template and go to the settings and turn on
    pages on the main then the root.
  </p>
  <h3>How to change content in a HTML page?</h3>
  <p>Look for the text between the opening and closing tags and change it.</p>
  <h3>How to change values in CSS?</h3>
  <p>
    Look for a selector and go to a property, after it there is the value that
    you can change.
  </p>
