  <h3>How do you wrap up a project?</h3>
  <p>
    Make a bunch of issues on github for what has to happen, as small as
    possible of issues. Assign people to them. You can create branches from
    issues and they will be auto linked to the pull request and auto close the
    issue.
  </p>
  <h3>How to structure a README</h3>
  <ol>
    <li>Title</li>
    <li>Description</li>
    <li>Usage</li>
    <li>Sources</li>
    <li>License</li>
  </ol>
