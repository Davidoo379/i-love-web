Managing expectations for your group members

Don't assume your teammates are more capable than they actually are, assume they don't know anything.

Be very careful with file names

Git is sensitive with capitals of file names where tracking refs can become a problem. First time you even commit a new file make sure the name follows conventions. No special characters or e with line on top like that.
