Managing expectations for your group members

Dont assume your teammates are more capable then they actually are, assume they dont know anything.

Be very careful with file names

Git is sentistive with capitals of file names where tracking refs can become a problem. First time you even commit a new file make sure the name follows conventions. No special characters or e with line on top like that.
