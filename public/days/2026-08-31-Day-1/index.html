What is the work model?

Analyze
Look at the assignment and make a breakdown and bulletpoints on what has the project has to have.
Design
Schets out the pages and there the structure with the content.
Build
Make the repository and write HTML, CSS
Deploy/integrate
Attach it to the other systems or pages it has and then publish you r site for all to see.
Test
Let others use your website and let them review your code. then collect all the feedback and go back to Analyze
